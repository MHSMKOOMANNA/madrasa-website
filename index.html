A Legacy of Knowledge & Faith

MAADANUDHEEN MADRASA is a cherished centre of Islamic learning in Koomanna Chenakkal, Kerala. We provide comprehensive education rooted in the Quran and Sunnah, shaping students with knowledge, character, and strong Islamic values.

Our dedicated teachers guide students through classical Islamic sciences alongside modern academic subjects, ensuring a holistic education for every child.
