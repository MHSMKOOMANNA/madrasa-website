About the 16th Batch

A sacred brotherhood forged over twelve transformative years of Islamic scholarship and spiritual upbringing.

❝
اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ

"Read in the name of your Lord who created."

— Surah Al-Alaq 96:1

We are the proud 16th Batch (WAFD) of Sabeelul Hidaya Islamic College (SHIC) — an esteemed institution fostering classical Islamic education synthesized with contemporary wisdom.

Our shared journey began on 9th May 2012, when 35 young aspirants stepped through the gates of SHIC. Over twelve demanding, enriching years, we immersed ourselves in the sciences of the Holy Quran, Hadith literature, Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh), Arabic rhetoric, and philosophy.

On 5th March 2024, we received our degree of Islamic scholarship at Darul Huda Islamic University (DHIU), stepping forth as dedicated ambassadors of truth, compassion, and community service.

Historical Chronology

Our 12-Year Academic Journey

A step-by-step evolution from young enthusiastic students to certified Islamic scholars.

📖
09 May 2012 Phase 01

The Noble Beginning

We officially entered Sabeelul Hidaya Islamic College as the 16th Batch. An eager cohort taking the first steps into the vast oceans of classical Islamic sciences.

Admissions Quran Recitation Arabic Foundations
🌱
2012 – 2016 Phase 02

Foundations & Language Mastery

Building mastery in classical Arabic grammar (Nahw & Sarf), Tajweed, Islamic jurisprudence basics, and Islamic history while cementing bonds of companionship.

Classical Arabic Tajweed Islamic Ethics
📿
2016 – 2020 Phase 03

Deepening Theological Sciences

Rigorous study of Hadith methodologies (Usool al-Hadith), Quranic exegesis (Tafseer), comparative Fiqh, logic (Mantiq), and classical theology (Aqeedah).

Hadith Sciences Tafseer Usool al-Fiqh
⭐
2020 – 2023 Phase 04

Scholarly Rigor & Discourse

Advanced academic research, master-level text analysis, eloquence in oratory, community leadership mentoring, and comprehensive university examinations.

Advanced Research Da'wah Training Scholarly Texts
